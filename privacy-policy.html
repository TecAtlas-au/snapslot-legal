Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 May 2026

This is a placeholder. SnapSlot is currently in pilot testing. A full privacy policy is being drafted and will be published before public launch. The summary below describes our current approach during the pilot phase.

Who we are

SnapSlot is a service operated by TecAtlas Pty Ltd (ACN 697 657 097), a company registered in Victoria, Australia. Our registered office is in Lara, Victoria.

What we collect

During pilot testing, SnapSlot collects only the information you provide when creating an account or using the app. This includes your email address, name, suburb, and any bookings or favourites you record. We also collect basic technical data (device type, app version, crash reports) to keep the app working reliably.

How we use it

Your information is used only to operate SnapSlot — to show you nearby slots, connect you with businesses, send booking confirmations, and improve the app. We do not sell your data and we do not share it with third parties for advertising purposes.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your personal data, ask us to correct it, or delete your account at any time. Account deletion details are available at our account deletion page.

Contact
