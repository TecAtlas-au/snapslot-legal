Account Deletion

Last updated: 14 May 2026

You can request deletion of your SnapSlot account and associated personal data at any time.

How to request deletion

To delete your account, send an email to hello@tecatlas.com.au with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" from the email address associated with your SnapSlot account.

Email hello@tecatlas.com.au

What gets deleted

  1. Your account profile (name, email, suburb, phone number if provided)
  2. Your booking history
  3. Your favourites and notification preferences
  4. Any reviews you have written
  5. Any business profile you have created (for business accounts)

What may be retained

For legal and accounting reasons, we may retain a record of completed financial transactions (including bookings where payment was processed) for the period required by Australian tax law. This retained data is not used to identify you and is held only as required by law.

Timeline

Account deletion requests are processed within 30 days of receipt. You will receive an email confirmation once your account has been deleted.

Contact

For any questions about account deletion, contact us at hello@tecatlas.com.au.
